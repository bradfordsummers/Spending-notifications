Privacy Policy — Spending Notifications

Last updated: July 2026

Spending Notifications is a private, personal tool operated by an individual to text a once-daily summary of their own household credit-card spending to themselves and their spouse. It is not a commercial product and has no other users.

Information we collect

We access the operator's own bank/credit-card transaction data through Plaid (a bank-connectivity provider), strictly for the card the operator personally linked. We also store the mobile phone numbers of the two household recipients so the daily message can be delivered.

How we use it

Transaction data is used only to calculate and send the daily spending summary (yesterday's purchases and progress toward a monthly budget) to the operator and their spouse. It is used for no other purpose.

How we share it

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, and we do not use it for marketing. Data is only transmitted between the operator's own systems, Plaid, and the messaging provider used to deliver the text. Bank login credentials are handled entirely by Plaid and are never seen or stored by this tool.

Data retention

The tool is stateless: it reads recent transactions at send time to compute the summary and does not maintain its own database of transaction history.

Opting out

A recipient can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, or by having their number removed from the tool's configuration.
