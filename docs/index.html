Spending Notifications

Spending Notifications is an automated alert service operated by Bradford Summers. Software connected to a subscriber's linked credit card generates one text message each morning summarizing the card's recent transactions and progress toward a monthly budget. Messages are generated and sent automatically by the application; they are not conversational and are not written or sent by a person.

What you'll receive

Content: a daily spending summary (transactional account alert), for example:

Spending Notifications $2,508 left 12 days to go 62% of $6,600 budget This month: $4,092 Yesterday: $247.83

Frequency: about 1 message per day.
Cost: message and data rates may apply.
Sender: (425) 399-7205.

How to subscribe

Text START to (425) 399-7205

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated daily spending-alert text messages from Spending Notifications at the number you texted from. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency: about 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

After texting START you'll get this confirmation:

"Spending Notifications: You're subscribed to daily spending alerts. About 1 msg/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You'll receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or email the contact below.
