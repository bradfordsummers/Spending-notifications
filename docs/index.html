Spending Notifications

Spending Notifications is a private, personal SMS program run by an individual for their own household. It sends the operator and their spouse a single automated text each morning summarizing their own household credit-card spending — the prior day's purchases and progress toward a monthly budget. It is non-commercial, has no customers, and sends to no one outside this two-person household.

Messaging use case

  • Who receives messages: only two people — the operator and their spouse.
  • What is sent: a personal account/spending summary (transactional).
  • How often: approximately one (1) message per day.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.

How recipients opt in (consent)

There is no public sign-up form. The two recipients are the operators of the tool themselves. Each recipient opts in by manually entering their own mobile number into the application's configuration and affirmatively agreeing to the consent statement below. Consent is never pre-selected, pre-checked, or assumed, and no number is ever added by anyone other than that number's owner.

Consent statement each recipient agrees to:

"I agree to receive recurring automated spending-summary text messages (about one per day) from Spending Notifications at the mobile number I have entered. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency is approximately 1 message per day. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe at any time. I have read the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions."

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe (you will receive one confirmation and no further messages). Reply HELP for assistance. A recipient's number can also be removed from the tool's configuration.

Disclosures

Information is never shared with third parties or used for marketing. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
