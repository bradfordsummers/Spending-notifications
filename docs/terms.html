Terms & Conditions — Spending Notifications

Last updated: July 2026

Program

Program name: Spending Notifications.
Description: A private, personal SMS program that sends the operator and their spouse a once-daily text summarizing their own household credit-card spending — the prior day's purchases and progress toward a monthly budget. There are no other recipients.

Message frequency

Approximately one (1) message per day.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the recipient's mobile plan.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for assistance. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe and stop receiving messages. After replying STOP you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts.
